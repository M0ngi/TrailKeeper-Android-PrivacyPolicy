# Trail Keeper Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Trail Keeper Android app handles information when you save places, set location alarms, review visits, search for addresses, or record routes.

## Information handled by the app

Depending on the features you use and the Android permissions you grant, Trail Keeper handles:

- **Location information:** Your current coordinates when you request your location or save your current position; coordinates and timestamps for a route while you explicitly record it; and entry or exit events used for saved-place visit history and location alarms. Background location is used for active route recording and for geofence-based visit and alarm features when you have granted background location permission.
- **Saved-place information:** Place names, icons, notes, coordinates, group assignments, alarm settings, and visit start and end times.
- **Route information:** Route names and recorded GPS points, which can include timestamps, altitude, accuracy, speed, and heading when provided by Android.
- **App preference:** Your selected light or dark appearance preference.

Trail Keeper does not require an account. The app does not send your saved-place database, visit history, or recorded routes to a Trail Keeper server. Its core saved-place and route data is stored in the app’s local database on your device.

## Location permission and background activity

You can use some features without granting location permission, but features that need your position will not work. If you enable alarms or visit tracking, Android may monitor geofence entry and exit events in the background. Route recording starts only after you choose to start it and requires foreground and background location permission. Android displays an ongoing notification while a route is recording. You can stop or pause recording in the app, and Android system controls may also stop its background service.

## Information shared with other services

Trail Keeper does not sell your personal information. It does not include its own advertising or analytics service. Some features make requests to services operated by others:

- **Google Maps:** The app uses the Google Maps SDK to display maps. Google receives the network and map requests needed to provide that service and may process technical information such as your IP address, device and app information, and map interaction or location information under Google’s terms and privacy policy. If you choose **Open in Google Maps** for a recorded route, the route’s start, end, and up to three sampled waypoint coordinates are included in the link opened by Google Maps. If you open a saved place in an external map app, that app receives the selected place’s coordinates.
- **Photon address search:** When you type at least three characters in map search, Trail Keeper sends the search text to the public Photon API operated by Komoot. If the app has your current coordinates, it also sends latitude and longitude to help prioritize nearby results. Because the request is made from your device, the service also receives network request information such as your IP address. Do not enter sensitive personal information in the search box. See [Komoot’s privacy policy](https://www.komoot.com/privacy) and the [Photon service](https://photon.komoot.io/).
- **Android notifications and sharing:** Alarms and recording controls use local Android notifications. When you export a place or route, Android’s share interface lets you choose another app or destination. That app or destination receives the file you choose to share and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Google’s services are covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and applicable [Google Maps Platform policies](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/android-sdk/policies). Photon search results use geographic data that includes OpenStreetMap data; see [OpenStreetMap’s copyright and license information](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

## Storage, backups, and security

Saved places, alarms, visit history, route data, and appearance preferences are stored in the app’s private storage on your Android device. The app does not add a separate encryption layer to its local database. Android app backup is enabled for Trail Keeper; depending on your Android version and backup settings, app data may be included in device backup or transferred during device migration. Those backups are managed by Android and your device account settings, not by Trail Keeper’s servers.

Network requests to Photon and Google services use HTTPS where provided by those services. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so protect access to your device and its backups.

## Retention and deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you delete it or remove Trail Keeper’s app data. You can delete saved places and routes in the app. Deleting a saved place also deletes its associated visit history and alarm data. Deleting a route removes its recorded points. You can clear the app’s data or uninstall it using Android settings; however, Android backups or files you previously exported or shared may remain in the destinations you chose and can potentially be restored from backup.

Google and Komoot handle information received through their services according to their own policies and retention practices. Contact those providers to exercise rights concerning information they process.

## Children’s privacy

Trail Keeper is a general-purpose location utility and is not designed to collect information from children. The app does not ask for a child’s name, account, or contact information.
